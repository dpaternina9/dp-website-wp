Hi, I'm David.

I'm a software developer and product builder in Colombia. I run Fanxie Lab, and I build my own iOS and SaaS products on the side of it.

Most of my work sits where performance, privacy, and the actual experience meet — the three things that get traded away first when a deadline shows up. I've spent enough years watching that trade get made to have opinions about it.

When I'm not writing code I'm pulling espresso shots, playing guitar through too many amp sims, or being judged by two cats. I write about some of that here.

The part I'm best at is scale that has to stay quiet.

For four years I worked on an analytics plugin running on around three million WordPress sites, in PHP, Vue.js, REST APIs and WP-CLI. At that size every change is a performance decision, and the interesting problem is never the feature — it's shipping it without anyone noticing you were there.

That habit carried into everything since. The rest of what I do, in short:

BACKEND

Laravel and NestJS on DigitalOcean and Cloudflare — API design, transactional email, R2 storage, provisioning. The unglamorous half that decides whether the rest works.

NATIVE iOS

Swift, SwiftUI and CloudKit. Apps that keep their data on the device and do not phone home, because that is the version I want to use.

FRONTEND

Vue.js and PHP at scale, and enough design work to know when an interface problem is really a product problem.

The long version of how I got here is a series I'm still writing, one part at a time. The short version is a timeline — roles, and the things that came out of them.
